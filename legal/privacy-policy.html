Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Racked ("we," "us," "our") operates the Racked mobile application (the "App"), a marketplace for buying and selling used powerlifting and strength-training equipment. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using Racked, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: your email address (used for sign-in via a one-time passcode — we do not store passwords).
  • Profile information: display name, profile photo, city and neighborhood, and whether you're primarily buying, selling, or both.
  • Listing information: when you post an item for sale, we collect the category, brand, title, description, price, condition, photos, and location (city/neighborhood) you provide.
  • Messages: the content of messages you send to other users through the in-app messaging system.
  • Ratings and reviews: ratings and written feedback you leave for other users after a transaction.
  • Reports: if you report a listing or another user, we collect the reason and any details you provide.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and push notification token: to deliver notifications (e.g. new messages), we collect a device-specific push token issued by Apple (APNs) or Google (FCM). This token does not by itself identify you personally.
  • Basic usage and diagnostic data: standard technical information (such as app version and device type) that our backend and error-monitoring tools may log to keep the App running reliably.

1.3 Information from photos

Photos you upload for a listing are stored so they can be displayed to other users, and are also processed by an automated content-moderation system (see Section 3) to help detect prohibited or inappropriate content before a listing goes live.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and profile
  • Display your listings to other users and let you browse others' listings
  • Enable messaging between buyers and sellers
  • Calculate and display buyer/seller ratings
  • Send you transactional notifications (e.g. new messages, listing status updates)
  • Review reported content and enforce our Terms of Service (including removing listings or restricting accounts that violate our rules)
  • Maintain and improve the security, stability, and functionality of the App

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

3. Automated Content Moderation

Photos submitted with new listings are automatically analyzed by a third-party AI vision service to flag content that may violate our content policies (for example, prohibited items or inappropriate imagery). Flagged listings are queued for human review by our moderation team before any final decision (such as removal) is made. This automated check does not make final enforcement decisions on its own.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With other users, as part of core functionality: your display name, profile photo, city/neighborhood, listings, and ratings are visible to other users of the App, since that's the point of a marketplace. Messages you send are visible to the recipient.
  • With service providers who help us operate the App, under contractual obligations to protect your data: database, authentication, and file storage hosting; email delivery (for sign-in codes and account-related emails); automated photo content-moderation; push notification delivery (Apple/Google infrastructure); app distribution and crash/error diagnostics (Apple/Google/Expo).
  • For legal reasons, if required to comply with a legal obligation, protect the rights and safety of our users, or investigate fraud or abuse.
  • In a business transfer, if Racked is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy or a successor policy.

We do not share your precise location, email address, or message content with other users beyond what's necessary for the marketplace to function (e.g. your city/neighborhood, not a precise address).

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and listing information for as long as your account is active. If you delete a listing, it's removed from public view; underlying records may be retained for a limited period for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and legal compliance purposes.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and update: you can view and edit your profile information directly in the App.
  • Delete a listing: you can remove your own listings at any time.
  • Block and report: you can block other users and report listings or messages you believe violate our policies.
  • Account deletion: to request deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact us at the email below.
  • Language: the App is available in English and Hebrew; you can change this in your profile settings at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

Racked is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encrypted connections and row-level access controls on our database) to protect your information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Users

Racked's infrastructure providers may process and store data outside your country of residence. By using the App, you consent to this transfer, storage, and processing.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by other reasonable means. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

support@rackedapp.com.de
