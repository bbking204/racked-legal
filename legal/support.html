Support

Need help with Racked, have a question about a listing or another user, or found a bug? We're here to help.

Email support support@rackedapp.com.de

Report abusive content or a user

Inside the app, every listing, wanted post, and user profile has a Report option, and every conversation has a ⋯ menu with Report and Block. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. You can also email us directly at the address above with details.

Account deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete Account inside the app. If you're unable to access the app, email us and we'll process the deletion within 30 days.
